I am a PhD student at [IRIF](https://www.irif.fr/) since October 2025. I am a member of 
the [Automata, Structures and Verification](https://www.irif.fr/en/poles/asv/index) 
research pole and the [Modélisation and Verification](https://www.irif.fr/en/equipes/verif/index) 
team, under the supervision of [Mo Foughali](https://mo-f.github.io/Mo-F/).
My research focuses on **Runtime Verification for real-time systems**, with the goal of developing 
efficient techniques for monitoring and reacting to property violations in safety-critical systems.
